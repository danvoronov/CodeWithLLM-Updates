**Uninstalling Muse Code**
https://github.com/TerryZH/muse-code-uninstaller
Unofficial MIT uninstaller for macOS in Bash. Dry-run by default, just calculates how much space will be freed. Only deletes with the `--uninstall` flag, leaves the parent `~/.local/bin` untouched. On Windows there is no entry in Add/Remove Programs, the installer writes itself to User PATH, plus it creates sandbox users `muse-sbx-r1` / `u1` and a `MuseSandboxUsers` group, profiles in `C:\Users\muse-sbx-*` and keys in `ProfileList` — after removal all of this has to be cleaned manually via `takeown /f "C:\Users\muse-sbx-r1" /r /d y`.
