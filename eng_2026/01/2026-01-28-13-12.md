**Agent Skills Adaptation**
Following Anthropic's rollout of the Skills API (`skills-2025-10-02`) and the release of the standard on December 18, 2025, OpenAI with GPT-5.2 Thinking [quietly responded](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Dec/12/openai-skills/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) almost immediately by adding `/home/oai/skills` to ChatGPT and `skills.md` support in Codex. MS integrated support into VS Code in December as well. and Cursor did too.
